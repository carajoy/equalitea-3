## Event-day experience
The page is designed for a guest using a phone during a live event.

Priorities:
1. Immediately identify the event.
2. Reach Today’s Program quickly.
3. See who is speaking now and who is next.
4. Identify panelists visually.
5. Reach speaker context without leaving the page.
6. Recognize sponsors without turning the page into a logo wall.
7. Preserve the Modern Historical Elegance theme without overwhelming the program.

## Theme
Modern Historical Elegance.

Use the suffrage-color meaning already established by the Chamber:
- Purple — Justice & Dignity
- White — Purity of Purpose
- Yellow — Hope and the Light of a New Day

## Mobile-first rules
- No pinch-to-zoom required.
- Readable type at normal phone scale.
- Short scanning distances.
- Clear section headings.
- Generous tap targets.
- Strong contrast.
- Avoid tiny sponsor logos.
- Avoid dense multi-column layouts on mobile.
- Keep page load fast.
